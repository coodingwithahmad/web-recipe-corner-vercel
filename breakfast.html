Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients

2 cups flour, 2 eggs, 1 1/2 cups milk, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp baking powder, butter.

Method

Whisk the dry ingredients. Stir in the eggs and milk until just combined. Cook portions on a buttered hot pan until golden. Serve with syrup and fruit.

Breakfast

Choco-Fruit Waffles

Ingredients

2 cups flour, 2 tbsp cocoa powder, 2 eggs, 1 3/4 cups milk, 1/3 cup sugar, berries.

Method

Mix the dry ingredients, then whisk in the eggs and milk. Cook in a hot waffle iron until crisp. Top with fruit and chocolate sauce.

Breakfast

Cheesy Egg Toast

Ingredients

2 slices bread, 1 egg, 1/2 cup shredded cheddar, 1 tbsp butter, salt and pepper.

Method

Butter and toast the bread in a pan. Add the egg and cheese, cover, and cook until set and melted. Season and serve.

Breakfast

Hash Browns

Ingredients

2 potatoes, 2 tbsp oil, 1/2 tsp salt, black pepper.

Method

Grate and squeeze the potatoes dry. Season and press into hot oil. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes per side until crisp and golden.
