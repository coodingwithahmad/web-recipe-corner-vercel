Cheesy Egg Toast

⏱ Prep: 5 mins | 🍳 Cook: 10 mins

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices bread
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter, salt & pepper

Instructions:

  1. Butter bread and toast one side lightly in a pan.
  2. Make an indent in the center, crack the egg inside, and surround with cheese.
  3. Cover and cook on low heat for 5 minutes until egg sets.

Garden Salad

⏱ Prep: 10 mins | 🍳 Cook: 0 mins

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups salad greens
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes & cucumbers
  • 2 tbsp olive oil & lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Toss greens, tomatoes, and cucumbers in a bowl.
  2. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice, then serve fresh.

Gourmet Dinner Roast

⏱ Prep: 15 mins | 🍳 Cook: 35 mins

Ingredients:

  • 500g roast protein
  • 3 cubed potatoes & 2 carrots
  • Garlic and rosemary sprigs

Instructions:

  1. Toss vegetables with oil and garlic.
  2. Roast everything at 190°C (375°F) for 35 minutes until tender.

Chocolate Lava Cake

⏱ Prep: 15 mins | 🍳 Cook: 12 mins

Ingredients:

  • 100g dark chocolate & 50g butter
  • 1 whole egg + 1 yolk
  • 2 tbsp sugar & 2 tbsp flour

Instructions:

  1. Melt chocolate and butter together.
  2. Fold in beaten eggs, sugar, and flour.
  3. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 10–12 minutes.
