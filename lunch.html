Grilled Cheese Salad

Ingredients

4 bread slices, 4 cheese slices, butter, lettuce, tomato, cucumber, lemon dressing.

Method

Make grilled cheese in a buttered pan until golden. Slice and serve over the salad vegetables with lemon dressing.

Mini Sandwiches

Ingredients

Slider rolls, sliced turkey, cheese, lettuce, tomato, mayonnaise.

Method

Spread mayonnaise on the rolls. Layer the turkey, cheese, lettuce, and tomato. Cut into small sandwiches and serve.

Creamy Pasta Salad

Ingredients

250 g pasta, mayonnaise, yogurt, cucumber, tomato, corn, salt and pepper.

Method

Cook and cool the pasta. Mix the dressing with the vegetables, fold in the pasta, and chill for 30 minutes.

Egg Avocado Salad

Ingredients

2 boiled eggs, 1 avocado, lemon juice, yogurt, salt and pepper.

Method

Chop the eggs and avocado. Fold together with lemon juice and yogurt. Season and serve on toast or salad leaves.
