Cheesy Egg Toast

By Recipe Corner • Updated May 22, 2026
10 minPrep
15 minCook
2Servings
380 kcalCalories

Golden toast, creamy melted cheese, and a soft cooked egg make this a fast favorite for busy mornings and simple brunches.

Chef's pro tip: Use a small glass to press a little well into the bread before adding the egg. This helps the egg stay centered and cook more evenly.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Lightly butter both sides of the bread and toast it in a warm pan for 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
  2. Press a small indent into the center of one slice and crack the egg into it.
  3. Sprinkle with cheese, cover the pan, and cook on low heat for 3 to 4 minutes.
  4. Flip gently or finish open-faced depending on your preferred doneness, then season with salt and pepper.
  5. Top with parsley and serve immediately with the second slice of toast.
