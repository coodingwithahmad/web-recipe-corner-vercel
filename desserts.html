Berry Glazed Cheesecake Slice

Ingredients

Biscuits, butter, cream cheese, sugar, eggs, mixed berries.

Method

Press crushed biscuits mixed with butter into a tin. Add the beaten filling and bake at 160 C until set. Cool and top with berries.

Chocolate Fudge Cupcake

Ingredients

Flour, cocoa, sugar, egg, milk, oil, baking powder, chocolate icing.

Method

Whisk the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Bake in cupcake cases at 180 C for 18 to 20 minutes. Cool and frost.

Chocolate Layer Cake

Ingredients

Flour, cocoa, sugar, eggs, milk, oil, baking soda, chocolate frosting.

Method

Divide the batter between two tins and bake at 180 C for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool, sandwich with frosting, and frost the outside.

Dessert

Glazed Chocolate Donuts

Ingredients

Flour, sugar, egg, milk, cocoa, baking powder, chocolate glaze.

Method

Spoon the batter into a greased donut pan. Bake at 180 C for 12 to 15 minutes. Cool, dip in glaze, and let it set.
