Dinner

Classic Steak and Fries

Ingredients

2 steaks, 3 potatoes, oil, garlic, salt and pepper.

Method

Season and sear the steaks in a hot pan. Cut potatoes into fries, toss with oil, and bake at 220 C until crisp. Rest the steak before serving.

Dinner

Grilled Chicken Souvlaki Platter

Ingredients

500 g chicken, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, pita, cucumber yogurt sauce.

Method

Marinate the chicken with oil, lemon, garlic, and oregano. Thread onto skewers and grill until cooked through. Serve with pita and sauce.

Dinner

Cheeseburger and Fries

Ingredients

500 g ground beef, 4 buns, cheese slices, lettuce, tomato, onion, potatoes.

Method

Shape and season the patties. Cook in a hot pan, add cheese, and toast the buns. Assemble with vegetables and serve with fries.

Dinner

Crispy Fried Chicken

Ingredients

4 chicken pieces, buttermilk, flour, paprika, salt, pepper, oil.

Method

Soak chicken in buttermilk for 30 minutes. Coat with seasoned flour and fry until golden and cooked through. Drain before serving.
