# 🍳 Recipe Corner — Multi-Page Food Recipe Platform

A multi-page food and culinary exploration website featuring recipe hubs, interactive utilities, cooking guides, and editorial articles.

## ✨ Features & Pages Breakdown

* **Categorized Meal Hubs:** Separate sections for `breakfast.html`, `lunch.html`, `dinner.html`, and `desserts.html` with designated banners and meal cards[cite: 3].
* **Quick & Healthy Lifestyle Hubs:** 
  * `quick-easy.html` for rapid meal ideas[cite: 3].
  * `healthy-diet.html` for nutrition-focused recipes and wholesome eating[cite: 3].
* **Kitchen Tools & Planning Utilities:**
  * `meal-planner.html` for planning daily and weekly meal routines[cite: 3].
  * `shopping-list.html` for organizing ingredient checklists[cite: 3].
  * `search.html` interface for finding dishes and recipes[cite: 3].
* **Editorial & Knowledge Section:**
  * `blog.html` and `blog-details.html` for culinary articles and food stories[cite: 3].
  * `cooking-tips.html` covering essential kitchen skills and food prep advice[cite: 3].
* **Community & Information:**
  * `submit-recipe.html` to share personal recipes[cite: 3].
  * `categories.html` & `recipes.html` for browsing collections[cite: 3].
  * `recipe-detail.html` for comprehensive single-recipe views[cite: 3].
  * `faq.html`, `about.html`, and `contact.html` for FAQs, platform story, and direct messages[cite: 3].
* **Responsive Layout Design:** Built with CSS Flexbox, Grid, and mobile-friendly toggle navigation patterns[cite: 3].

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

* **HTML5:** Semantic site structure (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`)[cite: 3].
* **CSS3:** Custom stylesheet system (`style.css`, `site.css`, and page-specific CSS files) utilizing Grid and Flexbox layouts[cite: 3].
* **Pure Vanilla Architecture:** Zero external frameworks or heavy dependencies[cite: 3].
